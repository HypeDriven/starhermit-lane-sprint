# Lane Sprint — canonical SFX manifest
# file | event id | description of the sound | usage context
# Event ids are the names passed to audio.playEvent() in src/audio.js. Each event
# owns a round-robin list of clips so repeated cues do not sound identical.
# Format: MOSS-SoundEffect v2.0, 48 kHz mono Opus (96 kbps VBR, loudness-normalized, 100 steps).

lane-whoosh-soft.opus | lane | A soft, quick airy whoosh of a small vehicle swishing sideways into an adjacent lane, short and clean. | Lane change accepted (rules.js applyAction, 'lane_left'/'lane_right').
lane-whoosh-quick.opus | lane | A fast, sharp whoosh of air as a racing pod darts sideways across the track, bright attack with a short tail. | Lane change accepted; second clip in the lane rotation.
lane-whoosh-deep.opus | lane | A low, deep whoosh of a heavy craft sliding one lane over, muffled rumble with a quick fade. | Lane change accepted; third clip in the lane rotation.
boost-ignition.opus | boost | A punchy rocket-style ignition burst from a boost engine, crackling flare-up followed by a rising roar. | Manual boost triggered by Space/W/Up or the Boost touch button.
boost-surge.opus | boost | A powerful turbine surge as a speed boost kicks in, accelerating jet-like roar building from low to high pitch. | Manual boost; second clip in the boost rotation.
boost-roar.opus | boost | A sustained aggressive engine roar of a racing vehicle at full boost, loud throttle growl with wind rushing past. | Manual boost; third clip in the boost rotation.
pad-chime.opus | pad | A bright crystalline pickup chime with a quick upward glissando, like collecting an energy pad, clean and sparkling. | Boost pad collected in rules.js step(); distinguishes free speed from a spent manual charge.
pad-charge.opus | pad | A short electric charge-up zap as a vehicle drives over a glowing power strip, fizzing energy with a bright pop. | Boost pad collected; second clip in the pad rotation.
checkpoint-blip.opus | checkpoint | A crisp two-note electronic blip confirming a checkpoint has been passed, clean synthetic tone, short. | Each of the four quarter-distance checkpoints crossed in rules.js step().
checkpoint-sweep.opus | checkpoint | A quick synthetic filter sweep rising then settling, a marker tone passing a track gate, short and airy. | Checkpoint crossed; second clip in the checkpoint rotation.
crash-impact.opus | crash | A heavy metallic crash impact of a vehicle slamming into an obstacle, loud clang with crunching debris. | Collision with traffic ends the run (rules.js step() sets crashed).
crash-scrape.opus | crash | A harsh grinding scrape of metal bodywork dragging along a barrier during a collision, screeching and rough. | Crash; second clip in the crash rotation.
crash-thud.opus | crash | A dull heavy thud of a vehicle body hitting the ground after a crash, short and weighty. | Crash; third clip in the crash rotation.
finish-fanfare.opus | finish | A short triumphant brass fanfare with a bright horn flourish as the racer crosses the finish line. | Finish line reached; plays as the results screen opens.
finish-chime.opus | finish | A bright sequence of rising bell chimes celebrating a race finish, clear and sparkling. | Finish; second clip in the finish rotation.
finish-applause.opus | finish | A small crowd applauding and cheering at a finish line, warm claps and excited shouts. | Finish; third clip in the finish rotation.
ui-select.opus | ui | A soft muted click with a subtle low tonal body, a menu button being selected, dry and understated. | Any non-gameplay action dispatched through handleAction() in src/index.js (menu, pause, settings, results).
ui-back.opus | ui | A soft descending two-tone click for backing out of a menu, muted and short. | Menu action; second clip in the ui rotation.
